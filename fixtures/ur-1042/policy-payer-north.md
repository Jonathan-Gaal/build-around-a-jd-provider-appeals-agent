SYNTHETIC POLICY

1. Concurrent review requests for continued inpatient psychiatric stay must be submitted before the last authorized day ends.
2. Requests must include progress notes for each day since the last review.
3. Continued stay requires documentation that the member continues to present a risk of harm to self or others, or a level of functional impairment, that cannot be safely managed at a lower level of care.
4. Requests must include a discharge plan with a target discharge date and the planned step-down level of care.
5. Payer North uses the LOCUS framework to review adult mental health levels of care.
6. Additional days are reviewed in increments of up to 3 days.
