# Job description

## The JD we build around

**Authorization (UR) Specialist, Ritten** (behavioral-health EHR company, hybrid in Philadelphia).
https://www.ritten.io/jobs/authorization-ur-specialist

It names the work this app automates:

- Prior authorizations and concurrent reviews
- Denials, appeals and peer-to-peer reviews
- Tracking denial trends and denial prevention
- ASAM criteria
- Levels of care: IOP, PHP, residential and detox
- Commercial, Medicaid and Medicare payers
